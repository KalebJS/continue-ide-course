## Design Decisions Made

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep navy (#1B2A4A) - conveys trust and professionalism
- **Secondary:** Warm gold (#D4A843) - adds warmth and premium feel
- **Accent:** Teal (#2DA68E) - for CTAs and interactive elements
- **Neutrals:** Light gray (#F5F5F5) background, dark gray (#333333) for body text

### Navigation Structure (Approved)
```
Home
About
  ├── Our Team
  ├── Our Story
  └── Careers
Services
  ├── Consulting
  ├── Implementation
  └── Support
Blog
Contact
```

### Homepage Layout
1. Hero section with dynamic background image and CTA: "Transform Your Business"
2. Three value proposition cards below the fold
3. Client testimonial carousel
4. Latest blog posts (3 cards)
5. Call-to-action banner before footer
